## 🏢 Civic Services

### ![](./.images/gov-uk-design-system-favicon.ico){.favicon} [GOV.UK Design System](https://design-system.service.gov.uk) (UK Government)

The gold standard. Whenever I'm unsure about usability best practices, it mostly
comes down to "what does GDS do?" for me.
